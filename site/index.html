A quiet place to write.

Glassine is a Markdown writing app for the Mac. A window of translucent glass, a caret that glides instead of jumps, and files that are plain text in iCloud Drive — saved as you type, yours to keep.

What it is

Fewer features than most writing apps, and fewer interruptions. Everything it does is on one screen or one key away.

And the rest

  • Glass. The window is a blur of whatever is behind it, tinted by the theme. Nine built in, dark by default; make your own with live preview.
  • A caret that glides. Speed, blink and width are yours to set. Off under Reduce Motion.
  • A library, not a file picker. Plain .md files and folders in iCloud Drive, named from the first line as you write.
  • Autosave that never asks. Half a second after you stop typing, and every few seconds while you keep going.
  • Tasks that behave. Click a box; the strike sweeps across; a moment later the item settles below the unfinished ones.
  • Dates as you type. @today becomes the date, drawn as a capsule, kept as text.
  • Markdown, lightly styled. Syntax stays visible but steps back — headings, emphasis, code, quotes, lists, links, tags.
  • Nothing sent anywhere. No accounts, no analytics, no servers. The privacy page is one paragraph.
