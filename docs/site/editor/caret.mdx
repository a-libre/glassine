The caret in Glassine is drawn by the app, not by the text system, so it can move the way a thing moves: when you press an arrow key, click somewhere, or type, it glides to the new position over about a quarter of a second, easing out, instead of vanishing from one place and appearing in another. It takes its colour from the theme — usually the accent — and its blink is a soft fade rather than an on-off flicker.

Everything about it is in Settings → Editor, under *Caret*.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Smooth movement | On | The glide itself. Off, the caret snaps like any other. |
| Glide time | 270 ms | How long a move takes, from 40 to 500 ms. Around 400 is where it stops reading as fast and starts reading as slow; past that it lags. |
| Smooth while typing | On | Whether the caret also glides from letter to letter as you type. Off, it glides for arrow keys and clicks but snaps instantly under your typing. |
| Blink | Soft | *Soft* fades in and out. *Classic* is the traditional on-off blink. *Never* holds steady. |
| Width | 4 pt | From 1 to 4 pt, in half-point steps. |
| Shape | Bar | One of eight; see below. |

## Shapes

Every shape is built on the same bar, so it glides, blinks and takes the width and colour you set the same way; what differs is what is drawn with it. Pick one in the row under *Width* — each is drawn there as it will look between two letters.

| Shape | What it is |
|---|---|
| Bar | The plain bar. |
| Pin | A bar with a round bead on top, like a pin in a map. |
| Serif | A bar with small feet at either end, the way an I is set in a serif face. |
| Wedge | A proofreader's caret — a small ^ — under the baseline, pointing up at the place. There is no bar at all; the mark sits in the space between lines. |
| Ghost | A faint block the width of the next letter, with a hairline at its left edge. At the end of a line, where there is no next letter, it is half an em wide. |
| Comet | A bar that trails a faint tail to its left, over the letters it has just passed. |
| Glow | A bar with a soft halo in its own colour. |
| Hollow | The outline of a bar, empty inside. |

If Reduce Motion is on in System Settings → Accessibility, gliding is turned off automatically and the caret behaves like a standard one. The same switch stills every other animation in the app; the [small delights page](/reference/small-delights) lists them.
