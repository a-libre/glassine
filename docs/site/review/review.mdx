⌘↩ (or ⌘⇧S) swaps the editor for a read-only rendering of the document. It is real HTML in a web view, so tables, nested lists, task lists, code blocks and images all appear the way another Markdown app would show them. Review comes in with a breath of scale; ⌘↩ or Esc returns to the editor at roughly the same place.

From All Documents, ⌘↩ opens the selected card straight into Review, and every right-click menu has *Open in Review*.

## Beside the editor

⌘⇧↩ (View → Review Beside the Editor, or *Review beside the editor* in the ⌘K command bar) keeps the page to the right of the text instead of in place of it. It is the same rendering, in the same style, and it is live: a beat after you pause typing the page takes the new text in place — no reload, no blink, the scroll where it was — and it scrolls to the block your caret is in, a third of the way down, as you move through the document. Drag the hairline between the two to share the width as you like; the share is kept, and so is the switch, between launches. ⌘⇧↩ again, or the × on the page's pill, puts it away. Esc stays the editor's. From Review, ⌘⇧↩ brings the editor back with the page beside it.

## Styles

Fourteen, chosen from the pill at the top-right of the page, from View → Review Style, or from the ⌘K command bar while you are in Review — or walked with ⇥ and ⇧⇥, next and previous. Most follow the theme, light or dark; the ones that bring their own paper stay as they are.

| Style |  |
|---|---|
| **Glass** | Your theme's colours, transparent over the blur. The default. |
| **Reader** | A quiet page the way a reading app sets one: a serif, a narrow measure, air between the lines. |
| **Gallery** | Wall text: small sans type, headings as spaced capitals, and white space as most of the page. |
| **Editorial** | Magazine typography: heavy headlines, pull-quotes. |
| **Newspaper** | A broadsheet: the headline across the page, two justified columns of small Times with a rule between them, a drop cap. One column when the page is narrow. |
| **Book** | A cream page with drop caps and small-cap headings. |
| **Book Dark** | The same page after dark: warm charcoal paper, cream type, a terracotta drop cap. |
| **Typewriter** | A manuscript: Courier on cream paper, double spaced, headings in underlined capitals, *emphasis* underlined the way a typewriter had to. |
| **Notebook** | Ruled paper with a red margin and blue-black ink; the writing keeps to the lines. |
| **Thesis** | A dissertation: Times, double spaced, sections numbered 1., 1.1, first lines indented, quotations in a single-spaced block. |
| **Verse** | For poems: centred, airy, an old-style serif. Every line is a line, and a blank line in the source is a stanza's space. |
| **GitHub** | github.com's rendering, light or dark to match the theme. |
| **Mono** | A terminal. |
| **Blueprint** | A drawing sheet: white line type on blueprint blue, a fine grid behind it, a double-ruled border. |

Headings are centered here too when *Center headings* is on in Settings.

## What is live on the page

**Task checkboxes.** Click one and the `[x]` is written straight into the file, with the same sweeping strike the editor draws.

**Links** open in your browser.

**Date capsules** are drawn around `@September 5, 2026` just as in the editor.

**Text size.** ⌘+ and ⌘− scale the page independently of the editor's type size; ⌘0 puts it back.

Review is also the style a [PDF export](/review/export-and-copy) is printed in.

<Note>
On the App Store version, Review is the reason the app holds the sandbox's outgoing-connection permission: the system web view cannot start without it. Nothing is sent; the [privacy page](/about/privacy) explains.
